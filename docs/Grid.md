## The horizontal grid (Arakawa C)

LLC4320 is an Arakawa C-grid ([MITgcm horizontal-grid
documentation](https://mitgcm.readthedocs.io/en/latest/algorithm/horiz-grid.html)):

- tracers at cell **centres** `(j, i)` — `Theta`, `Salt`, `CS`, `SN`,
  and every gradient output;
- `U` on the **west** cell faces `(j, i_g)`, together with `dxC`;
- `V` on the **south** cell faces `(j_g, i)`, together with `dyC`;
- vorticity naturally on the cell **corners** `(j_g, i_g)`.

![Arakawa C grid with ECCO naming and the tracer-gradient operation
path](images/grid_cgrid_schematic.png)

A finite difference moves a quantity by half a cell: differencing a
centre field lands on a face; differencing a face field lands on a
centre or a corner, depending on the direction.  That is why the
gradient routines interpolate at all — and why the ORDER of squaring
and interpolating matters.  See
[Gradients.md](Gradients.md#gradient--interpolation-artifacts).

## The vertical grid

Cell centres `Z` (dimension `k`) carry `Theta`, `Salt`, and `U`/`V` at
the same `k`; interfaces carry `W` (dimension `k_l`).  The interface
arrays overlap: `Zp1` is all `nk+1` interfaces, `Zl` is the low-index
(shallower) interface of each cell, `Zu` the high-index (deeper) one.

![MITgcm vertical grid: Z, Zl, Zu, Zp1, drF and drC, in dbof
positive-downward depth](images/grid_vertical_schematic.png)

Two things to keep straight:

- **`l` and `u` refer to INDEX POSITION, not depth.** `Zl[k]` is the
  SHALLOWER interface of cell `k`, `Zu[k]` the DEEPER one, in either
  sign convention.  This is the most common source of off-by-one
  errors when reading MITgcm vertical output.
- **Sign convention.** MITgcm/ECCO use a positive-UPWARD `Z`, so
  stored values are negative below the surface.  dbof works in
  positive-DOWNWARD depth: `vertical_helpers._get_depth_coord` flips
  the sign (and emits a `UserWarning` when it does).  Everything on
  this page is dbof depth unless it says "native".
- **`drF` vs `drC`.** `drF[k]` is the THICKNESS of cell `k`
  (`|Zu[k] - Zl[k]|`) and is the weight in every thickness-weighted
  vertical mean.  `drC` is the distance between adjacent cell
  CENTRES — used for `k_l`-level spacing, never as a thickness weight.

The depths in the figure are round illustrative numbers; the topology
is the point.  The real levels are below.
