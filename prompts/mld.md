# Generate/modify code related to the mixed layer depth (MLD)

## Goals

We wish to generate/modify code related to the mixed layer depth (MLD).  

## Context

The MLD is the depth to the bottom of the mixed layer.  It has many different definitions, but we will use the following definition as the default:

MLD = the depth where the potential density is 0.03 kg m^-3 greater than the potential density at 10m below the surface.

## Definitions

### Standard

Our standard definition for the MLD the depth where the potential density is 0.03 kg m^-3 greater than the potential density at 10m below the surface.

It is calculated by the mixed_layer_depth function in src/dbof/preprocessing/calculated_fields_at_depth.py.

### Isopycnal

Our isopycnal definition for the MLD the depth where the potential density is 0.125 kg m^-3 greater than the potential density at 10m below the surface.
